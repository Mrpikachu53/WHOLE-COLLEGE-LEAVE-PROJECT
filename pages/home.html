About the System

The First Hour Permission and Leave Approval System helps students submit applications online and allows faculty to review them through a dedicated dashboard. Students can track their applications, while faculty can approve or reject requests and provide remarks.

Our Features

Everything you need to manage permission and leave requests.

01

First Hour Permission

Request permission to arrive late by entering the date, expected arrival time, and your reason.

02

Leave Applications

Apply for leave with a start date, end date, and reason using a separate application form.

03

Request Tracking

Check whether your applications are pending, approved, or rejected from your student dashboard.

04

Faculty Approvals

Faculty can review student applications, approve or reject requests, and include remarks with their decisions.

05

Student & Faculty Access

Separate dashboards give students and faculty the tools they need after signing in to their accounts.

06

Request History

Students can view their previous applications, while faculty can review request summaries and export reports.
